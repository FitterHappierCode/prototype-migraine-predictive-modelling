Migraine and Headache Risk Prediction Prototype

This web-based prototype demonstrates two separate machine-learning models: a lifestyle-based migraine occurrence model and a headache diary trigger model.

This prototype is for educational and research purposes only. It is not a medical diagnosis tool.

Lifestyle-Based Migraine Occurrence Model

Uses sleep, mood, stress, hydration, screen time and date features from the original lifestyle dataset.

Enter Lifestyle Information

Prediction Result

Submit the form to see the latest migraine risk prediction.

Headache Diary Trigger Model

Uses a separate published smartphone headache diary dataset and predicts headache day risk from trigger-style diary inputs.

Reference dataset: Park et al. (2016), PLOS ONE smartphone headache diary study. This is used as the benchmark reference for diary variables and trigger categories.

Select Diary Factors

Tick the factors that apply to the diary day being considered.

Headache Diary Result

Submit the diary factors to see the headache day risk estimate.
